Vancouver Art Compass

A mobile app that boosts arts and cultural engagement in Vancouver by providing residents and visitors with reliable, up-to-date information on public art and local events.

This app has

All the things you need!

Get to know local artists

Discover and connect with Vancouver’s vibrant creative community. Our directory showcases local artists, from visual creators to performers, making it easy to explore their work and support their craft.

Discover public arts

Explore Vancouver’s public art scene like never before. Our interactive map lets you easily locate and learn about the city’s diverse public art installations, turning your city stroll into a cultural journey.

Find out what's happening around you

Stay up-to-date on the latest arts and cultural events around Vancouver. From exhibitions to festivals, our curated list ensures you never miss an opportunity to immerse yourself in the city’s dynamic arts scene.
